I am an investor that invests in and purchases small to medium businesses and I’m honored and excited that you are here!

Relationships are core for me and I live life focused a few principals:

1. Being in collaboration with others

– I buy and invest in businesses with a focus on the relationship

– I love to collaborate and work with people that are open minded

2. The quality of our relationships relate to the quality of our life

– Having an amazing life is relative to our efforts in how we show up

3. Treat others how I want others to treat me

– respect, dignity, being kind, compassion, understanding

– How we treat others is a reflection of how we see ourselves

4. The quality of our communication relates to the workability within our relationships

– through communication we can bond, we can understand one another

– through communication we come to agreement

5. Honor our word

– When we say we will do something and do it, this creates workability

– it reflects that we get the impact of our spoken word

6. Willingness to be solution oriented

– Life has its moments, it can be amazing, it can be challenging at times

– In the face of challenge it is necessary to seek solutions
